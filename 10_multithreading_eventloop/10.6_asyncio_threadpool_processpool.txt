| Tool                    | Best for         | Threads            | Parallel?                            | Why use it?                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   asyncio               | Non-blocking I/O | 1                  | No                                   | Efficiently manages thousands of waiting operations without extra threads. |
|   ThreadPoolExecutor    | Blocking I/O     | Many               | Not for Python CPU code (due to GIL) | Lets blocking functions run without freezing your main thread.             |
|   ProcessPoolExecutor   | CPU-bound work   | Separate processes | **Yes**                              | Bypasses the GIL for true multi-core execution.                            |
